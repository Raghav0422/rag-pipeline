Google, operating under its parent company Alphabet Inc., builds consumer and enterprise technology centered around information retrieval, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. Its core consumer platforms include Google Search, YouTube, Gmail, Google Maps, and the Android mobile operating system, which powers billions of smartphones globally. Revenue is primarily generated through digital advertising across Search and YouTube, alongside growing subscription services like Google One and YouTube Premium.

In artificial intelligence and cloud infrastructure, Google develops the Gemini family of multimodal models, including lightweight variants like Gemini 3.5 Flash designed for low-latency enterprise workloads. Through Google Cloud Platform (GCP), organizations access Vertex AI to build, fine-tune, and deploy machine learning models and autonomous enterprise agents. Unlike competitors that rely exclusively on third-party processors, Google designs custom Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) to train and run large-scale neural networks efficiently inside its global data centers.

Beyond software and cloud services, Alphabet invests heavily in long-term research and autonomous systems. Google DeepMind leads foundational research into reasoning models and physical world simulation systems such as Omni, while Waymo operates commercial autonomous ride-hailing fleets across major metropolitan areas.
