NVIDIA Corporation is a semiconductor and accelerated computing company specializing in graphics processing units (GPUs), high-speed data center interconnects, and AI software platforms. Originally founded to design GPUs for 3D video game rendering under the GeForce brand, NVIDIA's parallel processing architecture became the foundational hardware standard for training and running deep learning models.

In the data center market, NVIDIA provides full-stack AI factory infrastructure combining Blackwell and Hopper GPUs, Grace ARM-based CPUs, BlueField data processing units (DPUs), and Spectrum-X Ethernet networking. Multiple GPUs communicate at ultra-high bandwidth using NVLink and NVLink Switch technologies, allowing hyperscalers and enterprises to train massive transformer models across thousands of synchronized chips.

NVIDIA pairs its silicon with a proprietary software ecosystem led by CUDA, a parallel computing platform and programming model used by millions of developers. On top of CUDA, NVIDIA offers domain-specific libraries and microservices such as TensorRT for low-latency inference, NIM (NVIDIA Inference Microservices) for containerized model deployment, and Omniverse for industrial digital twins and robotics simulation.
